### Urban Mobility and Economic Analysis in Latin America

Cleaned, integrated and analyzed 2024 traffic and economic data from 15 cities across seven Latin American countries. Traffic observations were aggregated by city and year and combined with GDP per capita, population and other economic indicators through an INNER JOIN.

The analysis found no clear association between higher GDP per capita and greater average congestion. Mexico City was the main congestion outlier, with an average `jams_delay` close to 2,833 minutes, while population showed a more visible—but not uniform—association with traffic delay, congestion length and number of traffic jams.

Bogotá was identified as the city that best matched the transportation investment criteria: it recorded the highest traffic index in the sample, at 37.6, while its GDP per capita of USD 11,442 remained below the sample average and its population reached approximately 11.3 million residents. The project recommended considering Bogotá a priority for transportation infrastructure investment while recognizing that the analysis identifies descriptive patterns rather than causality.

**Skills:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, data cleaning, column standardization, type conversion, datetime processing, aggregation, INNER JOIN, exploratory data analysis, outlier detection, Matplotlib, Seaborn and business recommendations.

### MercadoLibre Funnel and Retention Analysis

Analyzed the conversion funnel for unique MercadoLibre users across ten Latin American countries between January and August 2025. The analysis measured six conversion stages, from product selection to purchase, and compared performance by country.

The largest drop occurred between `select_item` and `add_to_cart`: while 76.90% of users selected a product, only 11.01% added it to their cart, representing a decline of 65.89 percentage points. The overall purchase conversion rate was 1.25%. Uruguay and Chile recorded the strongest add-to-cart rates, while Argentina and Brazil showed the lowest results.

A retention analysis was also conducted at D7, D14, D21 and D28 by country and cohort. For users registered between January 1 and January 6, 2025, retention decreased from 86.2% at D7 to 3.0% at D28. Peru and Mexico recorded the highest D28 retention, while Colombia and Chile showed the lowest levels.

The project recommended improving the transition from product selection to cart addition, applying retention strategies between D7 and D14, and reviewing country-level differences to identify practices that could be replicated across markets.

**Skills:** SQL, CTEs, COUNT DISTINCT, CASE WHEN, DATE_TRUNC, TO_CHAR, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, retention analysis, country-level comparison, data validation and business recommendations.

### Financial Performance Analysis with SQL

Analyzed product financial performance across the United States, Australia, the United Kingdom, Germany, France and Canada using SQL query outputs and an executive dashboard. The analysis compared revenue, costs, campaign spending, gross profit, profit margin and marketing ROI to evaluate both product profitability and campaign efficiency.

Gross margins remained relatively stable across countries, ranging from approximately 41% to 45%, while ROI varied much more widely, from 17.43% to 75.75%. The United States generated the highest revenue, gross profit and ROI, whereas Canada recorded the lowest revenue and ROI. The United Kingdom also showed weak marketing efficiency because its high campaign spending did not produce a proportional return.

The project recommended prioritizing investment in the United States and reviewing campaign strategies in the United Kingdom and Canada to improve marketing efficiency.

**Skills:** SQL, aggregation, KPI calculation, revenue and cost analysis, gross profit, profit margin, marketing ROI, country-level comparison, scenario analysis, executive dashboards and business recommendations.

### Walmart Sales Executive Summary

Integrated and analyzed Walmart’s 2012 weekly sales data by combining store, department and sales tables into a cleaned dataset. Developed an interactive dashboard and executive summary using two business KPIs: sales per square meter to measure space efficiency and department share to evaluate each category’s contribution to total sales.

The analysis identified Grocery & Essentials as the strongest department, with USD 652.81 in sales per square meter and a 15.23% share of total weekly sales. Fresh Food and Household & Paper Goods also ranked among the top-performing categories. Together, the three leading departments represented 36.43% of total sales. The project recommended prioritizing inventory, store space and budget for high-performing departments, while reviewing promotions, assortment and space allocation for lower-contributing categories.

**Skills:** Google Sheets, data integration, lookup tables, data cleaning, quality validation, pivot tables, KPI development, interactive dashboards, sales analysis and executive reporting.

### Sales Data Cleaning and Analysis in Google Sheets

Cleaned and standardized a quarterly sales dataset by auditing duplicate records and missing values, correcting inconsistent city names, and separating product descriptions into category, type and specification fields.

Built an executive summary and visualizations covering approximately USD 2.94 million in sales across 753 transactions. The analysis identified Tablet as the best-selling product category, Monterrey as the city with the highest sales, and November as the strongest-performing month. Based on the results, the project recommended prioritizing tablet-focused marketing campaigns.

**Skills:** Google Sheets, data cleaning, missing-value auditing, text standardization, categorical data preparation, KPI calculation, sales analysis, data visualization and executive reporting.
