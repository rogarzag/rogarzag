## Data Analytics Projects

Selected projects showing how I connect business questions, data analysis, and practical recommendations.

### RappiPlus | Business Performance Analysis

Analyzed sales, profitability, conversion, retention, and a checkout A/B test using Python, SQL, statistical testing, and Tableau. Extreme-quantity records accounted for **81.45% of reported revenue**, so raw and adjusted views were assessed separately. The checkout experiment showed **no statistically significant improvement** (p = 0.4161).

**Business takeaway:** retain the existing checkout version, investigate friction between checkout and payment, and validate material data-quality issues before using results to support commercial changes.

**Tools:** Python · SQL · Tableau · Statistical testing

[View project repository](https://github.com/rogarzag/rappiplus-business-performance-analysis)

### Andes Capital | Real Estate Analysis

Built a Tableau story to examine revenue, property types, sales channels, customer segments, and repeat purchasing. The analysis covered **$6.01B in revenue and 8,500 sales**, with Brokers generating approximately **73% of revenue**.

**Business takeaway:** prioritize monitoring of the Broker channel and First-Time Buyers, and distinguish property types that lead revenue, ticket, or sales volume when setting commercial priorities.

**Tools:** Tableau · KPI analysis · Cohort analysis · Data storytelling

[View project repository](https://github.com/rogarzag/andes-capital-real-estate-analysis)

### Andes Retail | Commercial Performance Dashboard

Compared 2024–2025 sales through an interactive Tableau dashboard. Revenue declined **6.8% in 2025**, alongside fewer orders and lower average order value, while Home moved from the lowest-revenue category to the highest.

**Business takeaway:** investigate the decline through order volume and average order value, and review category and monthly performance before changing commercial priorities.

**Tools:** Tableau · KPI analysis · Dashboard design · Business communication

[View project repository](https://github.com/rogarzag/andes-retail-commercial-performance)

### MercadoLibre Funnel and Retention Analysis

Used SQL to assess the conversion funnel and D7, D14, D21, and D28 retention across ten Latin American countries. The largest funnel loss was a **65.89-percentage-point drop** from product selection (76.90%) to add-to-cart (11.01%). In the January 1–6 cohort, retention declined from **86.2% at D7 to 56.2% at D14**.

**Business takeaway:** investigate cart-add friction and prioritize early-retention actions.

**Skills:** SQL, CTEs, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, retention analysis and data validation.

[View project repository](https://github.com/rogarzag/mercadolibre-funnel-retention-analysis)
